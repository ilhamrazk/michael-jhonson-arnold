Executive Thesis

"The next frontier in mining productivity lies at the intersection of geotechnical risk mitigation and high-precision blast intelligence."

In open pit and quarry operations, drill and blast activities directly dictate rock mass stability, wall integrity, and hauling economics. Historically, geotechnical engineering and drill-and-blast operate in operational silos.

By combining millimeter-level radar deformation monitoring with drone-based computer vision for post-blast analytics, mining companies can eliminate blind spots: preventing fall-of-ground incidents, optimizing fragmentation, containing flyrock hazards, and safeguarding highwall design.
